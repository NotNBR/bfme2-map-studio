# The Hollin Dales

A 3 vs 3 map in autumn Eregion, 1092 x 754 cells.

Every player holds a closed dale of their own under the Misty Mountains, with
a wide bowl for the base. A team's three dales meet only in its gate-court;
the court opens onto the shared field through three gates. The field is the
ruined heart of Hollin: the Sirannon winds through it with two fords and a
central stone bridge through the ruins of Ost-in-Edhil. Dry hollows descend
between low spurs to uneven river terraces; broad, grassy shelves alternate
with steeper, exposed banks. Ground cover and groves follow those landforms.

The playable layout is point-symmetric (players 1-3 west, 4-6 east), and the
build refuses an unfair one; the cliffs, woods, ruins and rocks are not
mirrored, so the two halves look different.

```
mw dev hollin-dales       rebuild on every save, with the viewer open
mw play hollin-dales      install it into the game and start BFME2
```

`map.py` builds the map: heights, textures, objects, water and sky. See
[Making maps](../../docs/guide/projects-and-builds.md).
