# Brightwater Fold

A 2-player dale in Rohan, 480 x 480 cells.

```
mw dev brightwater-fold       rebuild on every save, with the viewer open
mw play brightwater-fold      install it into the game and start BFME2
```

- **Dale.** Open golden downs, paler on the crests and greener in the
  hollows, falling in long slopes to the river. Round hills crowned with rock
  tors stand in the wedges between the roads, with pine copses on their
  flanks, and Edoras rock outcrops dot the higher ground. Chalk escarpments
  flank the river where it enters at the north edge and leaves at the south,
  with a steep face where it cuts through them.
- **The Brightwater.** It parts round an island knoll in the middle of the
  map and meets again below it, flowing from the north edge to the south;
  the creeks flow into it. Each player's island road crosses an arm at
  a shallow gravel ford; the north and south roads cross the river on
  wooden bridges, with torches at each end.
- **The hold.** The knoll rises from the water up a rough rock face that
  rounds over into a level top. Its edge is uneven all round: coves and
  rocky shelves at the shore, buttresses and bays, rock ribs and grassy
  ledges on the face, with rock clusters, shrubs and pines clinging to the
  slopes and a small stand of pines on each tip. Outside the wall the knoll is
  scenery: impassable, except where the cuttings climb it. A curtain wall stands back from the edge with eight
  bastion towers, their tall carved fronts on the wall's face, stairs up
  from the courtyard and a flag on each; every run between two towers is
  the same length. Each gateway opens between two towers where the island
  road comes up a cutting from its ford. Two golden halls face each other across a plaza,
  with longhouses, lanterns, hay and stores round it.
- **What to hold.** A signal fire in the hold's plaza; an outpost with a
  small homestead for each player, about 1,600 from their start and 1,600
  further from the other; and a warg lair in each creek pocket. Each signal
  fire and outpost is taken at the capture flag beside it; the beacon's
  stands across the plaza from it, as far from either gateway.
- **Gollum.** Six spawn points for skirmish Gollum, three on each half out on
  the open downs.
- **Sound.** The Plains of Rohan bed, with the river and creeks, birds in the
  copses and on the downs, wind and falcons on the hills, voices in the hold
  and at the outposts and horses at the stables.
- **Creeks.** A creek runs in from beyond the west edge through its own
  gully into the river; the north road crosses it only on a footbridge. Its
  turned copy runs on player 2's side.
- **Paddocks.** A fenced horse paddock with a stable at its gate on the
  downs beside each base, with grazing herds, troughs, hay and a track to the
  road.
- **Light.** Warm late-afternoon sun with a cool fill from the sky.
- **Fairness.** The layout is point-symmetric about the centre. Both starts
  are level; the build measures the walk from each start to the hold, the
  other start and both outposts, and refuses an unfair result or an outpost
  that is not clearly its player's.

`map.py` builds the map. See [Making maps](../../docs/guide/projects-and-builds.md).
